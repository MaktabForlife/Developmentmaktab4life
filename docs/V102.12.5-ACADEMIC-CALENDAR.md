# V102.12.5 Academic Calendar

## User-facing terminology

- `Academic Calendar`
- `Holidays`
- `Islamic Dates`
- `Terms`

Internal identifiers remain unchanged (`AcademyCalendar`, `PUBLIC_HOLIDAY`) to avoid a Sheet migration.

## Islamic dates

Islamic dates are informational only. The UI shows the description, the Islamic date underneath it, the confirmed Gregorian date and Active/Inactive status. Alternate dates and TeachingImpact are not exposed in the UI. Legacy values may remain in the Sheet but do not change timetable teaching status.

## Holidays

South African statutory holidays continue to be generated automatically, including Good Friday, Family Day and the Sunday-following-Monday rule. Their default description is `Public Holiday`.

Admin may edit the Holiday description and date inline, remove a holiday with `×`, or add another holiday with `+`. Saved exceptions use the existing `AcademyCalendar` tab and therefore require no schema change.
