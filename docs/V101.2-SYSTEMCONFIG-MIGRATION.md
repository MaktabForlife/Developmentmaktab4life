# V101.2 SystemConfig migration

## Purpose

V101.2 makes `SystemConfig` a settings-only sheet, moves the global Zoom link
there, and removes the Worker's dependency on every `Next...Number` row.

## Safe deployment order

1. Make a dated backup of the development or production Google Sheet.
2. Deploy the V101.2 Worker/backend while leaving `SystemConfig` and
   `TimeTable` unchanged.
3. Verify Student, Admin, Subject, Task, StudentTask and resource IDs are now
   generated from their authoritative data sheets.
4. Deploy the V101.2 frontend.
5. Sign in with an `ADMIN` account and open **System Settings**.
6. Enter the existing global Zoom URL in **Global Zoom link** and save all
   settings. This creates or updates `GlobalZoomLink` in `SystemConfig`.
7. Reload Student and Admin timetables and verify the global Join Zoom action.
8. Verify at least one session-specific `TeacherAssign.ZoomLink` still opens
   its own meeting rather than the global meeting.
9. Only after those checks pass, remove the counter rows listed below.

## Counter rows that may then be removed

- `NextStudentNumber`
- `NextAdminNumber`
- `NextSubjectNumber`
- `NextResourceNumber`
- `NextTaskNumber`
- `NextStudentTaskNumber`
- `NextTaskResourceNumber`
- `NexteBookNumber`
- `NextPrintableNumber`
- `NextAudioNumber`
- `NextVideoNumber`
- `NextOtherResourceNumber`
- `NextModuleNumber`

Do not remove the settings rows:

- `StudentLoginBaseUrl`
- `WeeklyPlannerDriveFolderId`
- `WeeklyPlannerDriveFolderLabel`
- `GlobalZoomLink`

## Compatibility and rollback

- Until `GlobalZoomLink` exists, timetable reads fall back to the first legacy
  `TimeTable.ZoomLink` value.
- The old `/api/admin/timetable/update-zoom` endpoint remains temporarily for
  cached frontend compatibility, but it writes `SystemConfig`, not `TimeTable`.
- Do not delete the `TimeTable` or `TeacherAssign` tabs in this release.
- Rolling back to a pre-V101.2 Worker after removing counters requires restoring
  the counter rows from the backup first.
- No Apps Script deployment or new Worker binding is required.
