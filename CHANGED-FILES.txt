Important boundaries
1. Complete V102.2 account migration before testing V102.3 central login.
2. Keep Platform schema `102.0.3`; no Sheet header change is required.
3. Keep both PLATFORM_SPREADSHEET_ID and GOOGLE_SPREADSHEET_ID.
4. Deploy Worker and Pages to development before production.
5. Test `/account/<uniqueid>` with migrated test accounts.
6. Keep `/admin/<uniqueid>` and `/student/<uniqueid>` operational.
7. Central account tokens remain blocked from legacy course-data endpoints.
8. Keep every course Library and resource record course-specific.
9. Keep TeacherAssign and TimeTable as the live timetable source.

Rollback
Reverting the Worker and Pages to V102.2 removes the central account route/API
without affecting legacy operational logins. Central account rows and updated
LastLoginDate, LastUsedDate or upgraded PIN hashes may remain safely.
