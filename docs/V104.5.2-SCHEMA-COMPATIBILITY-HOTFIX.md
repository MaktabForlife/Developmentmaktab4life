# V104.5.2 — Platform Schema Compatibility Hotfix

## Problem

V104.5.1 correctly advances the Platform workbook to `PlatformSchemaVersion 102.0.11`, but three older runtime guards were still capped at `102.0.9`:

- central account authentication/revalidation (`/api/account/check` and central account state loading);
- Academic Calendar administration;
- central account migration preview/verification.

After the Course scheduling migration, `/api/account/check` could therefore return HTTP `503` even though the Platform workbook itself was valid. Browser consoles surface this as a failed `check` resource.

## Fix

V104.5.2 extends those compatibility guards to accept the current staged Platform schemas:

- Central account auth/revalidation: `102.0.3` through `102.0.11`.
- Central account migration tooling: `102.0.3` through `102.0.11`.
- Academic Calendar: `102.0.8`, `102.0.9`, `102.0.10`, `102.0.11`.

No Sheet columns, tabs, business rules, access rules, Course scheduling rules, publication rules or cache behaviour are changed.

## Regression protection

The existing behavioural tests now explicitly prove:

1. `/api/account/check` succeeds at `PlatformSchemaVersion 102.0.11`.
2. Academic Calendar get/save paths remain valid at `102.0.11`.
3. Central account migration preview remains available at `102.0.11` for an already-current migration state.
4. The complete backend suite remains green.

## Deployment

This is a code-only hotfix. If the Platform workbook is already at `102.0.11`, do **not** rerun Prepare Scheduling and do not manually alter the workbook. Deploy the V104.5.2 Worker/app overlay and verify central account login plus Academy Calendar.
